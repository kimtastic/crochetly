The bind off (also called cast off) secures your stitches so they cannot unravel. Different bind-off methods produce different edges: some are stretchy (ideal for socks and hat brims), some are decorative (for visible edges), and some are designed to match specific stitch patterns. This guide covers the most common methods grouped by what they do best.

## Standard bind offs

These are the most common all-purpose bind-off methods. They work for most projects and are the first ones beginners learn.

### Basic knit bind off

Knit two stitches, then lift the first stitch over the second and off the needle. Knit one more stitch and repeat. Creates a neat edge but has very little stretch.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=knitting+basic+knit+bind+off)

### Basic purl bind off

Same as the knit bind off but worked on the purl side. Purl two stitches, lift the first over the second, purl one more, and repeat. Good for finishing reverse stockinette edges.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=knitting+basic+purl+bind+off)

## Stretchy bind offs

These methods produce an elastic edge that can stretch as much as the fabric does. Use them for socks, hat brims, cuffs, and any edge that needs to go over something wider than the bound-off row.

### Suspended bind off

Knit one stitch, then knit the next. Pass the first stitch over the second, but do not drop it from the left needle yet. Knit another stitch, drop the first two stitches together, pass the next over. This creates extra slack between stitches.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=knitting+suspended+bind+off)

### Yarn over bind off

Knit one stitch, yarn over, knit another stitch, then pass both the yarn over and the first knit stitch over the second stitch. Repeat across. The yarn overs add slack for extra stretch.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=knitting+yarn+over+bind+off)

### Two row bind off

Work an entire row of yarn overs (yo, k1 across), then on the next row bind off normally (lift stitches over). The extra row of yarn overs creates a very elastic edge.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=knitting+two+row+bind+off)

## Shaped bind offs

These methods create a curved or diagonal edge. Use them for shoulder shaping, necklines, and anywhere you need the bind off to follow a contour.

### Decrease bind off

Work a decrease (k2tog or p2tog) before binding off each stitch. The decreases gradually reduce the stitch count, creating a gentle curve. Useful for rounded necklines and armholes.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=knitting+decrease+bind+off)

### Sloped bind off

Work short rows across the stitches before binding off, so the edge slopes gradually instead of stepping. Creates a smooth diagonal line for shoulders without the stair-step look of a standard bind off.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=knitting+sloped+bind+off+shoulder)

## Decorative bind offs

These methods add visual interest to the edge. Use them for shawls, blankets, cuffs, and any edge that will be visible.

### Picot bind off

Bind off 2 stitches, then cast on 2 stitches using the backwards loop method. Bind off 4 stitches (the 2 new plus the next 2). Repeat across. The small loops (picots) create a decorative scalloped edge.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=knitting+picot+bind+off)

### One-over-two bind off

Knit three stitches, then lift the first stitch over the next two and off the needle. Knit one more stitch, lift the previous over. Creates a thicker, ridged edge that stands out from the fabric.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=knitting+one+over+two+bind+off)

### Single crochet bind off

Using a crochet hook, work slip stitches across the needle, one through each knit stitch. Creates a clean, sturdy, non-curling edge. Popular for steeking and for finishing garter stitch.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=knitting+single+crochet+bind+off)

### Double crochet bind off

Same as single crochet bind off but using double crochet stitches. Creates a thicker, more decorative edge with more drape than the single crochet version.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=knitting+double+crochet+bind+off)

## Ribbed bind offs

These methods are worked in pattern to match ribbing. They maintain the stretch of the ribbed fabric and create a tidy edge that blends with the stitch pattern.

### K1, P1 bind off

Work each stitch in pattern (knit the knits, purl the purls) while binding off. When you bind off a knit stitch, lift the previous stitch over it. When you bind off a purl stitch, lift the previous stitch over that. Matches 1x1 ribbing.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=knitting+k1p1+bind+off+ribbing)

### K2, P2 bind off

Same as K1, P1 bind off but working in pairs. Knit and bind off two knit stitches, then purl and bind off two purl stitches. Matches 2x2 ribbing and wider rib patterns.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=knitting+k2p2+bind+off+ribbing)

## Seamed bind offs

These methods join two pieces of fabric together while binding off, or use grafting to create an invisible edge.

### Three needle bind off

Place the live stitches of both pieces on separate needles held parallel. Using a third needle, knit one stitch from the front needle together with one from the back needle. Knit another pair, then lift the first stitch over the second. Repeat across. Creates a firm seam that joins the pieces as you bind off.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=knitting+three+needle+bind+off)

### Sewn bind off (grafting)

Use a tapestry needle to weave the yarn through the stitches, mirroring the path of a knit row. Creates an invisible, stretchy edge that looks like an unbroken row of knitting. Covered in detail on the [Kitchener stitch](/knitting-reference/kitchener-stitch/) page.

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=knitting+sewn+bind+off+kitchener)
