Use this guide to find standard measurements for mittens, fingerless mitts, and gloves. Hand measurements vary more than other body measurements, so size charts are approximate. Always check fit against a real hand when possible.

## What are the standard mitten sizes?

Mitten sizes range from child (5 in hand circumference) to adult extra large (10.5 in). Total length and thumb length also vary by size.

| Size | Hand Circumference | Total Length | Thumb Length | Notes |
|------|--------------------|--------------|--------------|-------|
| Child (0-12 mo) | 4.5-5 in (11.5-12.5 cm) | 4-5 in (10-12.5 cm) | 1-1.5 in (2.5-4 cm) | |
| Child (1-3 yr) | 5-5.5 in (12.5-14 cm) | 5-6 in (12.5-15 cm) | 1.5-1.75 in (4-4.5 cm) | |
| Child (4-6 yr) | 5.5-6 in (14-15 cm) | 6-7 in (15-18 cm) | 1.75-2 in (4.5-5 cm) | |
| Child (7-10 yr) | 6-6.5 in (15-16.5 cm) | 7-8 in (18-20 cm) | 2-2.25 in (5-5.5 cm) | |
| Youth | 6.5-7 in (16.5-18 cm) | 8-9 in (20-23 cm) | 2.25-2.5 in (5.5-6.5 cm) | |
| Adult S (Women's 5-7) | 7-7.5 in (18-19 cm) | 9-10 in (23-25.5 cm) | 2.5-2.75 in (6.5-7 cm) | |
| Adult M (Women's 8-10 / Men's 7-8) | 7.5-8.5 in (19-21.5 cm) | 10-11 in (25.5-28 cm) | 2.75-3 in (7-7.5 cm) | Most common size |
| Adult L (Men's 9-11) | 8.5-9.5 in (21.5-24 cm) | 11-12 in (28-30.5 cm) | 3-3.25 in (7.5-8 cm) | |
| Adult XL (Men's 12-14) | 9.5-10.5 in (24-26.5 cm) | 12-13 in (30.5-33 cm) | 3.25-3.5 in (8-9 cm) | |

For a comfortable fit, design mitten circumference 0.5-1 in (1-2.5 cm) larger than hand circumference for positive ease. Mittens need more room than socks because hands are less compressible.

## What ease works best for mittens?

Unlike socks, mittens use zero to slight positive ease. A mitten that is too tight restricts movement and makes the hand cold.

- **Fingerless mitts:** 0-0.5 in (0-1 cm) positive ease. Close fit keeps them in place.
- **Full mittens:** 0.5-1 in (1-2.5 cm) positive ease. Room for warmth and movement.
- **Lined mittens:** 1-1.5 in (2.5-4 cm) positive ease on the outer shell to accommodate the lining.
- **Stranded colorwork mittens:** 0.5-1 in (1-2.5 cm) positive ease. Colorwork fabric is denser and less stretchy, so it needs more room.
- **Ribbed cuffs:** 0-0.5 in (0-1 cm) negative ease at the wrist to keep mittens in place. Ribbing stretches, so the cuff can be slightly smaller than the wrist.

## What are the standard glove sizes?

Gloves need a closer fit than mittens because each finger is separate. Use these measurements for five-finger gloves.

| Size | Hand Circumference | Palm Length | Thumb Length | Index Finger Length |
|------|--------------------|-------------|--------------|---------------------|
| Child (4-6) | 5.5-6 in (14-15 cm) | 2.5-3 in (6.5-7.5 cm) | 1.5-1.75 in (4-4.5 cm) | 1.5-1.75 in (4-4.5 cm) |
| Child (7-10) | 6-6.5 in (15-16.5 cm) | 3-3.25 in (7.5-8 cm) | 1.75-2 in (4.5-5 cm) | 1.75-2 in (4.5-5 cm) |
| Youth | 6.5-7 in (16.5-18 cm) | 3.25-3.5 in (8-9 cm) | 2-2.25 in (5-5.5 cm) | 2-2.25 in (5-5.5 cm) |
| Adult S | 7-7.5 in (18-19 cm) | 3.5-3.75 in (9-9.5 cm) | 2.25-2.5 in (5.5-6.5 cm) | 2.25-2.5 in (5.5-6.5 cm) |
| Adult M | 7.5-8.5 in (19-21.5 cm) | 3.75-4 in (9.5-10 cm) | 2.5-2.75 in (6.5-7 cm) | 2.5-2.75 in (6.5-7 cm) |
| Adult L | 8.5-9.5 in (21.5-24 cm) | 4-4.25 in (10-11 cm) | 2.75-3 in (7-7.5 cm) | 2.75-3 in (7-7.5 cm) |
| Adult XL | 9.5-10.5 in (24-26.5 cm) | 4.25-4.5 in (11-11.5 cm) | 3-3.25 in (7.5-8 cm) | 3-3.25 in (7.5-8 cm) |

For gloves, design with 0-0.25 in (0-0.5 cm) positive ease at the palm. Fingers need minimal ease. Too much fabric bunches inside outer gloves or interferes with dexterity.

## How do I measure hand size for mittens or gloves?

Measure the dominant hand (right if right-handed). Take measurements over bare skin for the most accurate fit.

- **Hand circumference:** measure around the widest part of the palm, just below the thumb, excluding the thumb.
- **Total length:** measure from the tip of the middle finger to the base of the palm (or to the wrist for longer mittens).
- **Thumb length:** measure from the thumb crotch (where the thumb meets the palm) to the tip of the thumb.
- **Palm length:** measure from the base of the palm (wrist crease) to the base of the middle finger.
- **Finger length:** measure from the finger crotch to the fingertip for each finger individually.
