## What is Crochetly?
Crochetly is a free, educational crochet resource. It’s designed to help crocheters learn techniques, understand stitches, and explore pattern design concepts through guides, references, and tools.

Crochetly is not a shop or marketplace.

---

## Is Crochetly an ecommerce site?
No. Crochetly does **not** sell crochet patterns, yarn, hooks, kits, or finished items.

There is no checkout, no digital downloads for sale, and no physical products available on this site.

---

## Do you sell crochet patterns?
No. Crochetly does not sell, license, or distribute crochet patterns.

Some pages may explain how patterns work, provide links to designers, show examples, or provide tools to help you design your own patterns, but Crochetly itself does not offer patterns for purchase or download.

---

## Can I buy yarn or crochet supplies here?
No. Crochetly is not a yarn store or supply shop.

We do not sell yarn, hooks, needles, accessories, kits, or any physical crochet materials.

---

## Is Crochetly affiliated with any yarn brands or pattern sellers?
No. Crochetly is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with yarn companies, pattern marketplaces, or online shops.

Any external links to third-party sites are provided for reference or learning purposes only.

---

## Is Crochetly free to use?
Yes. All Crochetly content is free to access.

There are no subscriptions, paid memberships, or premium features required to use the site.

---

## Who is Crochetly for?
Crochetly is for:
- Beginners learning the basics of crochet
- Intermediate crocheters expanding their skills
- Advanced crocheters interested in stitch theory and pattern design
- Anyone looking for clear explanations and reference material

---

## Can I use Crochetly to design my own patterns?
Yes. Crochetly includes educational content and tools intended to help you understand stitch structures, charts, and design concepts so you can create your own patterns.

Crochetly does not claim ownership over patterns you design using the site.

---

## Does Crochetly provide customer support for purchases?
No. Because Crochetly does not sell products or services, we do not provide support related to purchases, payments, refunds, or downloads.

---

## I saw a site selling “Crochetly” patterns or products. Is that you?
No. Crochetly does not sell products and does not authorize others to sell products on its behalf.

If you encounter a site claiming to sell Crochetly patterns, yarn, or kits, it is not affiliated with this project.

---

## How can I contact Crochetly?
If you have questions, feedback, or suggestions related to the educational content on this site, you can contact us through the methods listed on the Contact page.

Please note that we cannot assist with purchasing patterns or supplies from third-party websites.

---

## Will Crochetly sell patterns or supplies in the future?
At this time, Crochetly is focused solely on providing free educational resources and tools. There are no plans to operate as an ecommerce site.
