The Crochet Guild of America (CGOA) Master Crochet program helps experienced crocheters take their skills to the next level. It is not a beginner course. It is a self-guided program that tests your knowledge, technique, and attention to detail through a portfolio of work.

**Pricing note:** Costs listed on this page are as of July 2026. Check the CGOA site for current pricing.

**Source:** [Crochet Guild of America](https://www.crochet.org/become-a-master/)

## What is the CGOA Master Crochet program?

The CGOA offers two independent master programs: Fundamentals of Crochet and Advanced Stitches and Techniques. You can complete them in any order, though CGOA recommends finishing one before starting the other. Each program results in a certificate and recognition as a CGOA Certified Master.

The program is open to both CGOA members and non-members. There is no completion deadline. You work at your own pace and submit your portfolio when you are ready.

## Who should become a master crocheter?

The master program is for crocheters who want to deepen their understanding of the craft, not just complete more projects. You should consider it if:

- You have been crocheting for a while and want to move beyond following patterns
- You want to understand how stitches work and why techniques matter
- You are interested in teaching, writing patterns, or tech editing
- You enjoy challenging yourself with structured, self-guided learning
- You want formal recognition of your skills

This is not a program for beginners. You should be comfortable reading patterns, working basic and intermediate stitches, and maintaining consistent gauge before you start.

## What does the Fundamentals program cover?

The Fundamentals of Crochet program is a 54-page instructional workbook. It is designed for all skill levels and focuses on the concepts and principles behind crochet.

The program includes:

- **17 swatches** that demonstrate specific techniques
- **36 questions** about the swatches and the material presented
- **1 master project** that pulls together everything you learned

The workbook is divided into eight chapters:

- **Pattern Reading** - abbreviations, terms, and methods for understanding written patterns
- **Beginning Chains** - different approaches including foundation stitches
- **Circles** - working in the round, joining methods, increases, circumference
- **Basic Stitch Locations** - front and back loops, post stitches, spike stitches, yarn overs, turning chains
- **Gauge** - measuring gauge and its effect on drape
- **Weaving In Ends** - five different finishing techniques
- **Blocking** - six approaches to blocking
- **Masters Project** - a pattern that highlights skills from previous chapters

## What does the Advanced Stitches program cover?

The Advanced Stitches and Techniques program is a 27-page program for intermediate to advanced crocheters. It tests your mastery of advanced techniques.

The program includes:

- **48 swatches** covering advanced stitch patterns
- **Multiple questions** testing your knowledge
- Written explanations of techniques

The program is divided into seven parts:

- **Getting Started** - program overview and expectations
- **Gauge** - understanding gauge, including the Golden Loop method for adjusting row gauge
- **Special Stitches** - extended stitches and linked stitches
- **Textured Stitches** - front and back loops, post stitches, popcorns, clusters, puff stitches, cables, shells, fans, bullion stitches, picots
- **Stitch Patterns** - 10 advanced decorative stitch patterns with written instructions but no photos
- **Motifs** - square, triangle, pentagon, hexagon, dimensional flower
- **Final Touches** - edgings including reverse single crochet and braids

## How does the portfolio review work?

When you finish your workbook and swatches, you submit your portfolio for review. The Masters Committee assigns a reviewer who evaluates your work against the program standards.

If your portfolio has 5 or more unsatisfactory items, it is considered not passing. You can rework those items and purchase a second review. Additional reviews must happen between 1 month and 1 year after the previous review.

If your portfolio passes, you receive a written review, a certificate, and a pin. Your name is added to the list of CGOA Master Crochet graduates.

## How do I start?

1. Go to the [CGOA Masters Program page](https://www.crochet.org/become-a-master/) and read the program descriptions.
2. Choose which program to start with (Fundamentals or Advanced).
3. Purchase the program workbook from the [CGOA store](https://members.crochet.org/store/).
4. Work through the workbook at your own pace. There is no deadline.
5. When you are ready, submit the [Masters Candidate Form](https://members.crochet.org/portfolio-form) to have your portfolio reviewed.
6. Mail your portfolio to the assigned reviewer and wait for feedback.

You can find support and community through the [CGOA Masters Program group on Ravelry](https://www.ravelry.com/groups/cgoa-masters-program) and through CGOA chapter meetings.
