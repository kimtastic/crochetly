Body measurements describe the wearer's actual size, not the finished garment. When choosing a pattern size, compare your body measurements to the pattern's size chart, then add or subtract ease to get the fit you want.

## Fit and ease

Ease is the difference between your body measurement and the finished garment measurement. The amount of ease determines how the garment fits.

### Bust / Chest Fit and Ease Chart

| Fit | Ease | Finished garment vs bust or chest |
|-----|------|----------------------------------|
| Very close fitting | Negative ease | 2–4 in (5–10 cm) less than actual measurement |
| Close fitting | Zero ease | Same as actual measurement |
| Classic fit | Positive ease | 2–4 in (5–10 cm) more than actual measurement |
| Loose fit | Positive ease | 4–6 in (10–15 cm) more than actual measurement |
| Oversized | Positive ease | 6+ in (15+ cm) more than actual measurement |

A stretchy stitch pattern like ribbing needs less positive ease than a firm stitch pattern like stockinette. Always check the finished garment measurements in the pattern, not just the size label.

## How to measure yourself

Take body measurements over undergarments, not over bulky clothing. Use a flexible measuring tape and keep it parallel to the floor.

- **Chest:** measure at the fullest part, keeping the tape level across the back.
- **Waist:** measure at the narrowest point, typically where your body bends to the side.
- **Hips:** measure at the widest point around the hips and seat.
- **Cross back:** measure across the back from shoulder bone to shoulder bone.
- **Back waist length:** measure from the prominent bone at the base of the neck down to the waist.
- **Back hip length (men):** measure from the prominent bone at the base of the neck down to the top of the hipbone.
- **Center back neck-to-wrist:** measure from the center of the back of the neck, across the shoulder, and down the arm to the wrist bone.
- **Arm length to underarm:** measure from the shoulder point to the underarm crease.
- **Upper arm:** measure around the widest part of the upper arm, just below the armpit.
- **Armhole depth:** measure from the shoulder point straight down to the underarm crease.
