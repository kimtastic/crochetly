---
title: Care symbols
description: Reference for international care symbols used on yarn labels and in patterns, covering washing, bleaching, drying, ironing, and dry cleaning instructions with standard symbols and meanings.
---

Yarn labels use international care symbols to show how to wash and care for the finished item. These symbols follow ISO standards and are recognized worldwide. Understanding them helps you keep your projects looking their best.

**Source:** [Craft Yarn Council](https://www.craftyarncouncil.com/standards) care symbols standards

## What are the standard washing symbols?

Washing symbols show whether a yarn can be machine washed, hand washed, or not washed at all. The icon uses a washtub shape with variations.

| Symbol | Name | Meaning |
|--------|------|---------|
| Washtub with one dot | Machine wash cold | Machine wash at or below 30 C (85 F) |
| Washtub with two dots | Machine wash warm | Machine wash at or below 40 C (105 F) |
| Washtub with three dots | Machine wash hot | Machine wash at or below 50 C (120 F) |
| Washtub with hand | Hand wash | Hand wash in lukewarm water, do not machine wash |
| Washtub with X over it | Do not wash | Do not wash. Take to a dry cleaner. |
| Washtub with bar under it | Permanent press | Machine wash with cold rinse, reduced spin |

## What do the bleaching symbols mean?

Bleaching symbols use a triangle shape. Only one variant is common: non-chlorine bleach on delicate fibers.

| Symbol | Name | Meaning |
|--------|------|---------|
| Triangle | Bleach allowed | Any bleach may be used when needed |
| Triangle with lines | Non-chlorine bleach only | Only color-safe (non-chlorine) bleach |
| Triangle with X | Do not bleach | No bleach of any kind |

## What are the drying symbols?

Drying symbols use a square shape with variations for tumble drying, line drying, and flat drying.

| Symbol | Name | Meaning |
|--------|------|---------|
| Square with circle | Tumble dry allowed | Machine drying is safe |
| Square with circle and one dot | Tumble dry low | Tumble dry at low heat setting |
| Square with circle and two dots | Tumble dry medium | Tumble dry at medium heat setting |
| Square with circle and three dots | Tumble dry high | Tumble dry at high heat setting |
| Square with circle and X | Do not tumble dry | Do not machine dry |
| Square with three vertical lines | Drip dry | Hang wet, do not wring or squeeze |
| Square with one horizontal line | Line dry | Hang to dry on a clothesline |
| Square with two horizontal lines | Dry flat | Lay flat to dry, do not hang |
| Square with curved lines | Dry in shade | Protect from direct sunlight |

## What ironing symbols should I know?

Ironing symbols use an iron shape. The temperature is indicated by dots: one dot for cool, two for warm, three for hot.

| Symbol | Name | Meaning |
|--------|------|---------|
| Iron | Iron allowed | Ironing is safe at any temperature |
| Iron with one dot | Cool iron | Iron at low temperature (110 C / 230 F) |
| Iron with two dots | Warm iron | Iron at medium temperature (150 C / 300 F) |
| Iron with three dots | Hot iron | Iron at high temperature (200 C / 390 F) |
| Iron with X | Do not iron | Do not iron or press |
| Iron with lines | Do not steam | Iron without steam |

## What about dry cleaning symbols?

Dry cleaning symbols use a circle. Most craft yarns do not require dry cleaning, but acrylic and some specialty blends may list it.

| Symbol | Name | Meaning |
|--------|------|---------|
| Circle | Dry clean | Professional dry cleaning is safe |
| Circle with X | Do not dry clean | Do not dry clean |
| Circle with letter | Dry clean method | Letters indicate the solvent type (P, F, W) |

## Where do care symbols appear on yarn labels?

Care symbols are printed on the yarn label, usually near the bottom or on a separate care strip. Some manufacturers include them as a row of icons, others use a small chart. The order is always the same: wash, bleach, dry, iron, dry clean.

If the label uses text instead of symbols, check the wording for specific instructions like "machine washable" or "hand wash only."

## How should I include care instructions in my patterns?

List the care symbols or text instructions near the materials section so makers know how to treat the finished item. If you are not sure which symbols apply, check the yarn label of the sample yarn and include the same care information. If the pattern allows any yarn weight or fiber, include a general note like "check your yarn label for care instructions."
