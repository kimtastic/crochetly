## Where can I find crochet video tutorials?

**Answer:** YouTube has the largest collection of free crochet tutorials. Search for any technique, stitch, or project and you will find step-by-step video guides.

- [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=crochet+tutorials) — search for any crochet technique or stitch

## Where can I find crochet patterns?

**Answer:** Ravelry is the largest crochet and knitting pattern database with thousands of free and paid patterns. Amigurumi Today specializes in free amigurumi patterns. Etsy offers both paid and free patterns from independent designers.

- [Ravelry](https://www.ravelry.com/) — the biggest crochet and knitting pattern database
- [Amigurumi Today](https://amigurumi.today/) — free amigurumi patterns and tutorials
- [Etsy](https://www.etsy.com/) — paid and free patterns from independent designers
- YouTube tutorials for video guides

## Where can I find crochet help and community?

**Answer:** Ask for help in crochet Facebook groups, Reddit communities (r/crochet), or Ravelry forums. These are great places for troubleshooting and inspiration.

## Where can I find crochet supplies?

**Answer:** Local yarn shops, craft stores (Michael's, Joann), and online retailers (LoveCrafts, Hobbii, KnitPicks) all carry crochet supplies and yarn.
