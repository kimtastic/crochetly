Crochetly is a free, educational crochet and knitting resource, not a shop or marketplace. There are no products for sale, no subscriptions, and no downloads. Just clear instruction to help you learn and improve.

## About me

I'm Kim, known online as JesterKim.

I started Crochetly because I wanted a crochet reference site that was straightforward, ad-free, and focused entirely on teaching. No shops, no affiliate links, no fluff. Just clear explanations.

Crochet has been a creative outlet for me for years, and I wanted to share the techniques and knowledge I have picked up along the way. Over time, it became clear that many crocheters also knit, so Crochetly expanded to cover knitting too.

You can find me on [Ravelry](https://www.ravelry.com/people/JesterKim) and [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/jesterkimknits/).
