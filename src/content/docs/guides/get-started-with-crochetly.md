Crochetly is a free educational resource for crochet and knitting. There are no shops, no sales, and no ads. Just clear guides, references, and tools to help you learn and improve.

## How is Crochetly organized?

The sidebar on the left is your main navigation. Content is grouped into a few key areas:

- **Start here**. Beginner guides for [crochet](/guides/get-started/), [knitting](/guides-knitting/get-started/), [amigurumi](/guides/amigurumi-get-started/), and [Tunisian crochet](/guides/tunisian-get-started/)
- **Terms and measurements**. [Terminology tables](/terms/crochet-terminology/), gauge guides, and swatching help for both crafts
- **Abbreviations**. Full [abbreviation references](/abbreviations/crochet/abbreviations-us/) in US, UK, French, Spanish, Japanese, and multilingual
- **Reference**. Guides for [reading patterns](/reference/reading-patterns/), [choosing yarn](/reference/reading-yarn-labels/), [blocking](/terms/blocking/), and troubleshooting
- **Tools and Apps**. [Interactive tools](/apps/) and community resources

Pick the craft you are interested in and start browsing. Every page works on its own, so you can jump to whatever you need.

## Why does Crochetly cover both crochet and knitting?

Crochet and knitting are closely related crafts. Many makers do both, and techniques, yarn knowledge, and problem-solving skills carry over between them. Covering both crafts makes Crochetly a more complete resource for everyone, whether you are just learning one or already do both.

If you only crochet or only knit, you can safely ignore the other craft's sections. They are clearly labeled in the sidebar.

Look for these sections in the sidebar:

## Can I use this site on mobile?

Yes. Crochetly works on phones, tablets, and desktops. The sidebar collapses on smaller screens. Tap the menu icon to open it.

## Is there a search feature?

Yes. Click the search icon (or press `Ctrl+K` / `Cmd+K`) to search across all pages. The search bar is at the top of every page.

## Are there external links?

Some pages link to useful resources outside Crochetly, like YouTube tutorials, Ravelry, and Craft Yarn Council standards. These open in a new tab and are clearly marked.

## Do I need an account?

No. Everything on Crochetly is free and accessible without signing up.
