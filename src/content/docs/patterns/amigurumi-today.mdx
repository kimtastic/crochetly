**Sources:** [Amigurumi Today](https://amigurumi.today/)

Amigurumi Today is a website and mobile app dedicated to free amigurumi patterns. New patterns are posted weekly, ranging from beginner-friendly projects to more advanced designs. It is one of the most popular sources for free amigurumi patterns online.


## How do I access Amigurumi Today?

Amigurumi Today is available as a website and a free mobile app. Both offer the same patterns.

- A website at [amigurumi.today](https://amigurumi.today/)
- A free iOS and Android app (search "Amigurumi Today" in your app store)

The website and app offer the same patterns. The app provides a more mobile-friendly browsing experience with bookmarking.

## What types of amigurumi patterns are available?

Patterns cover a wide variety of amigurumi projects for all skill levels.

- Animals (bears, cats, bunnies, octopuses, birds, and many more)
- Dolls and characters
- Keychains and small gifts
- Seasonal and holiday projects
- Home decor items

Patterns are designed for various skill levels, from beginner to advanced. Each pattern page indicates the skill level.

## What does each Amigurumi Today pattern include?

Every pattern includes:

- Materials list (yarn weight, hook size, stuffing, notions)
- Step-by-step written instructions
- Photos of the finished project from different angles
- Tips and notes from the designer

Most patterns use standard crochet abbreviations and US terminology.

## How do I bookmark patterns on Amigurumi Today?

On the app, you can bookmark patterns to save them for later. This is useful for building a collection of projects you want to make.
