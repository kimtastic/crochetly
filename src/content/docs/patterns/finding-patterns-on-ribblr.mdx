Ribblr is a modern crafting platform featuring interactive ePatterns. It offers both free and paid patterns for crochet, knitting, and sewing. For a full guide on using Ribblr's features, including account setup, the ePattern format, Ribbuild for designers, and community, see the [Using Ribblr](/apps/using-ribblr/) page.

This page focuses specifically on finding patterns.


**Sources:** [Ribblr](https://ribblr.com/)

## How do I browse patterns on Ribblr?

Ribblr's home feed shows trending and recommended patterns based on your interests.

You can also:

- Search by keyword for specific projects
- Browse by category (amigurumi, garments, accessories, home decor, etc.)
- Filter by craft type (crochet, knitting, sewing)
- Filter by skill level

## How do daily free patterns work on Ribblr?

Ribblr offers free patterns on a daily rotation. You can claim a limited number of free patterns each day.

This is a good way to build your pattern library without spending money, but check back regularly, as the selection changes.

## What is the ePattern format on Ribblr?

Ribblr patterns use the ePattern format, which is different from traditional PDFs and works best within the Ribblr app or website.

The ePattern format includes:

- Progress tracking (mark rows or sections as complete)
- Smart sizing (select your size and the pattern adjusts)
- Built-in translation
- Video tutorials embedded in the pattern
- Interactive charts and diagrams

## How do I buy patterns on Ribblr?

Paid patterns are purchased directly through Ribblr and stored in your library.

Your purchased patterns are stored in your library and available anytime. You can also find free patterns from the daily selection and from designers who offer free designs.

## What tips should I know for using Ribblr patterns?

- **Daily free patterns:** Check the app each day for the free pattern selection
- **Progress tracking:** ePatterns save your progress automatically, so you can pick up where you left off
- **Smart sizing:** If you are between sizes, the ePattern format adjusts the instructions to your chosen size
- **See the full guide:** For help with account setup, Ribbuild, selling patterns, and community features, visit [Using Ribblr](/apps/using-ribblr/)

## Frequently asked questions about Ribblr patterns

### Are Ribblr patterns free?

Ribblr offers both free and paid patterns. Free patterns are available on a daily rotation, and some designers offer free designs. Paid patterns are purchased directly through the platform.

### Do I need the Ribblr app to use ePatterns?

The ePattern format works best within the Ribblr app or website. It includes interactive features like progress tracking and smart sizing that do not work in standard PDF readers.

### Can I sell items made from Ribblr patterns?

It depends on the individual pattern's license. Check the pattern listing or contact the designer for their commercial use policy.
