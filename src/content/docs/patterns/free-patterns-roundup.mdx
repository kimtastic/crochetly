Free crochet patterns are available from many sources beyond specific apps and marketplaces. This page covers where to find them and how to build a collection of free patterns you love.


## Where can I find free crochet patterns from yarn brands?

Major yarn brands offer free patterns to promote their products. These patterns are professionally written and tested.

Popular sources include:

- **Yarnspirations:** Free patterns from brands like Bernat, Caron, and Lily Sugar'n Cream
- **Lion Brand:** A large library of free patterns sorted by yarn weight and project type
- **Red Heart:** Hundreds of free patterns for all skill levels
- **Hobbii:** Free patterns alongside their yarn catalog
- **DROPS Design:** Free patterns from Garnstudio with detailed instructions and diagrams

Yarn company patterns often use their own yarns, but you can usually substitute with similar weights from other brands.

## Where do independent designers share free patterns?

Many independent designers offer free patterns on their blogs or websites. Free patterns are often used to introduce new designers or to promote paid pattern collections.

Search for patterns by visiting designer websites directly or following them on social media.

## How do I get free patterns through newsletters?

Signing up for designer newsletters is one of the best ways to get free patterns. Many designers offer a free exclusive pattern as a welcome gift when you subscribe.

Newsletters also notify you about new free patterns, sales, and pattern releases.

## Can I find free crochet patterns on social media?

Yes. Social media is a rich source of free patterns shared by designers and the crochet community.

- **Instagram:** Search for hashtags like #freepattern, #freecrochetpattern, or #crochetfreebie
- **Pinterest:** Pin boards dedicated to free crochet patterns are easy to browse
- **YouTube:** Many crochet YouTubers share free patterns alongside video tutorials
- **Facebook:** Crochet groups often share links to free patterns

## Which pattern marketplaces have free crochet patterns?

Several platforms offer both free and paid patterns. Filtering by price or availability helps you find the free options.

- **Ravelry:** Filter by "free" to access thousands of free patterns — see [Finding patterns on Ravelry](/patterns/finding-patterns-on-ravelry/)
- **Etsy:** Filter by price $0 to find free PDF patterns — see [Finding patterns on Etsy](/patterns/finding-patterns-on-etsy/)
- **Ribblr:** Daily free patterns available through the app — see [Finding patterns on Ribblr](/patterns/finding-patterns-on-ribblr/)
- **Amigurumi Today:** Dedicated to free amigurumi patterns — see [Amigurumi Today](/patterns/amigurumi-today/)

## How do I build a free crochet pattern collection?

Use these strategies to grow and organize your free pattern library.

- **Save links:** Bookmark patterns you like rather than downloading everything — patterns sometimes get updated
- **Check the license:** Free does not always mean you can sell finished items made from the pattern. See [Pattern licensing](/patterns/pattern-licensing/) for more information
- **Download PDFs:** For patterns you plan to make soon, download the PDF to your device or cloud storage
- **Test before committing:** Make a small swatch or a test square before starting a full project with a new-to-you designer

## Frequently asked questions about free crochet patterns

### Are free crochet patterns legal to use?

Yes, as long as you follow the designer's license terms. Free patterns are still copyrighted — you cannot share the PDF or sell finished items unless the license allows it.

### Where is the best place to find free crochet patterns?

Yarn company websites and Ravelry have the largest collections of free patterns. Designer newsletters are great for exclusive freebies not available elsewhere.

### Do I need an account to download free patterns?

It depends on the source. Ravelry requires a free account. Yarn company websites and designer blogs usually let you download without an account. Etsy requires an account to purchase even $0 patterns.
