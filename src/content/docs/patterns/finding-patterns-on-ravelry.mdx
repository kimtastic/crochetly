This page focuses specifically on finding patterns.


## How do I search for patterns on Ravelry?

Type keywords related to the project you want, like "granny square blanket" or "crochet sweater." Ravelry searches pattern names, descriptions, and designer names.

## What advanced search filters does Ravelry have?

Ravelry's Advanced Search gives you powerful filtering options to narrow down patterns by craft, yarn weight, difficulty, and more.

| Filter | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Craft | Choose crochet, knitting, or both |
| Availability | Free patterns, paid patterns, or both |
| Yarn weight | Lace, fingering, sport, DK, worsted, bulky, super bulky |
| Yardage | Minimum and maximum yarn required |
| Hook size | Filter by specific hook sizes |
| Difficulty | Beginner to expert |
| Category | Garments, accessories, home decor, toys, and more |
| Attributes | Seamless, worked in rounds, colorwork, lace, etc. |

You can combine multiple filters to narrow results precisely. For example: crochet, free patterns, worsted weight yarn, beginner difficulty, blankets.

## How do I browse patterns by designer on Ravelry?

Visit a designer's page to see all of their patterns in one place. Many designers organize their patterns into collections. For example, "Beginner-Friendly Patterns" or "Seasonal Projects."

## What information is on a Ravelry pattern page?

Each pattern page shows:

- Photos of the finished project
- Yarn requirements (brand, weight, yardage)
- Hook size
- Gauge
- Difficulty level
- Projects made by other Ravelry users
- Reviews and ratings
- A link to purchase or download the pattern

## How do I save patterns on Ravelry?

When you find a pattern you like, you can:

- **Favorite it:** Bookmark the pattern to find it later
- **Add to queue:** Mark it as a pattern you plan to make
- **Add to library:** Organize patterns into custom categories

## What tips should I know for finding patterns on Ravelry?

- **Free patterns:** Filter by "Free" availability to find thousands of free crochet patterns
- **Project photos:** Browse project pages to see how a pattern looks in different yarns and colors
- **Yardage calculator:** Ravelry tracks how much yarn other people used, which is useful for planning your purchase
- **See the full guide:** For help with account setup, project tracking, stash management, and forums, visit [Using Ravelry](/apps/using-ravelry/)

## Frequently asked questions about finding patterns on Ravelry

### Do I need an account to search patterns on Ravelry?

Yes. You need a free Ravelry account to browse the pattern database, use search filters, and save patterns.

### Are patterns on Ravelry free?

Ravelry has both free and paid patterns. You can filter by "Free" in Advanced Search to see only free patterns.

### Can I see what a pattern looks like in different yarns?

Yes. Each pattern page shows projects made by other Ravelry users, so you can see how it looks in different yarns and colors.
