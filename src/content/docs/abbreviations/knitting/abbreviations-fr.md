**Reading a knitting pattern in French?** This guide covers the most common French knitting abbreviations.

French knitting terminology uses `m end` (maille endroit) for knit and `m env` (maille envers) for purl. Designers may use their own abbreviations, so always check the pattern's key before you start.

**Sources:** [Craft Yarn Council](https://www.craftyarncouncil.com/standards/abbreviations), French knitting publications
