**Reading a knitting pattern in Japanese?** This guide covers common Japanese knitting abbreviations in romaji and characters.

Japanese knitting patterns use a mix of katakana loanwords and native Japanese terms. Many patterns also use schematic-style charts where symbols represent stitches. Always check the pattern's key for the exact symbols used.

**Sources:** Japanese knitting publications, [Craft Yarn Council](https://www.craftyarncouncil.com/standards/abbreviations)
