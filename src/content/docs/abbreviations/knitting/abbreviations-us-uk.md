**Need to decode a knitting pattern?** This reference covers the standard US and UK knitting abbreviations from `k` (knit) to `ssk` (slip slip knit), organized by category.

Unlike crochet, where US and UK use different names for the same stitch (e.g. US `sc` = UK `dc`), knitting terminology is consistent across both regions. The abbreviations `k`, `p`, `yo`, `k2tog`, `ssk`, and all other standard knitting abbreviations mean the same thing in US and UK patterns. The only differences are minor spelling variations (color vs colour, meter vs metre) that do not affect the abbreviations.

Designers and publishers sometimes use special abbreviations in a pattern. Always check the pattern's key before you start.

**Sources:** [Craft Yarn Council](https://www.craftyarncouncil.com/standards/abbreviations)
