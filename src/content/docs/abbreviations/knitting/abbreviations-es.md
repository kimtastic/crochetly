**Reading a knitting pattern in Spanish?** This guide covers the most common Spanish knitting abbreviations.

Spanish knitting terminology varies by region; Spain and Latin America may use different terms for the same technique. Always check the pattern's key (clave de abreviaturas) for the exact terms.

**Sources:** [Craft Yarn Council](https://www.craftyarncouncil.com/standards/abbreviations), Spanish knitting publications
