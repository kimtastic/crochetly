Crochet Row Counter and Patterns (also called Crochet App) is an all-in-one app that combines pattern storage, row counting, stash management, and a shopping list. It also includes a curated selection of built-in patterns designed for mobile viewing.

**Sources:** [Crochet Row Counter and Patterns](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/crochet-row-counter-patterns/id1545599414)

## How do I get started with Crochet Row Counter and Patterns?

Download Crochet Row Counter and Patterns from the App Store or Google Play. The app is free with in-app purchases for premium features like cloud sync and unlimited storage.

## How do I track rows in the app?

Track rows with simple plus and minus controls. Use multiple counters within a single project to track different sections. A built-in timer tracks how long each part takes.

## Can I import PDF patterns?

Upload PDF patterns from other apps, marketplaces like Etsy, or pattern databases like Ravelry. The app stores them so you can view them alongside your row counters.

The app also supports a mobile-optimized pattern format designed to be easy to read on small screens.

## What built-in patterns come with the app?

The app includes over 15 free crochet and knitting patterns optimized for mobile reading. New patterns are added regularly from top creators. These patterns are formatted specifically for the app and include progress tracking.

## How do I manage my yarn stash?

Log your yarn collection by brand, weight, color, and quantity. The stash manager includes yarns from popular manufacturers like YarnArt, Drops, Alize, Schachenmayr, and DMC. You can also track your crochet hooks and other tools.

## How does the shopping list work?

Plan upcoming projects with a built-in shopping list. Add yarn, fiberfill, hooks, and other supplies you need to buy. The shopping list syncs to the cloud with the premium subscription.

## What project planning tools are available?

- **Completion time planner:** Estimate how long a project will take
- **Hook and needle size charts:** Quick reference for standard sizes
- **Abbreviation guide:** Look up stitch abbreviations without leaving the app

## Does the app support cloud sync?

Premium users can sync patterns, counters, and shopping lists across devices. Your data is backed up and available on any device where you are signed in.

## What are the best tips for this app?

- **Start with built-in patterns.** They are formatted for mobile and include progress tracking.
- **Import PDFs from Etsy or Ravelry.** Use the row counter alongside your imported patterns.
- **Use the stash manager.** Track what you own and avoid duplicate purchases.
- **Plan with the shopping list.** Check off items as you buy them.
- **Use the timer.** Track how long each section takes to estimate future projects.
