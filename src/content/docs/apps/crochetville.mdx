Crochetville is a crochet community built around a blog and discussion forum. It offers a warm, old-school space for crocheters to connect, share projects, ask questions, and discover designers. Unlike large social platforms, Crochetville keeps a focused, positive community feel.

**Sources:** [Crochetville](https://www.crochetville.com/)

## How do I get started on Crochetville?

You can browse the blog without an account. To participate in the forum, you will need to sign up.

- **Create a forum account.** Visit [forum.crochetville.com](https://forum.crochetville.com/) and register with your email.
- **Set up your profile.** Add a photo and a short bio. Forum members are friendly and appreciate seeing who they are talking to.
- **Introduce yourself.** Most forums have an introductions section. Saying hello is a good way to start.

## What does Crochetville offer?

Crochetville has two main areas: the blog and the forum.

- **Blog.** Articles about crochet techniques, designer interviews, project ideas, and community news. Updated regularly with fresh content.
- **Forum.** Discussion boards where members post questions, share WIPs and FOs, discuss techniques, and chat about crochet life.
- **NatCroMo Blog Tour.** An annual March event featuring a virtual tour of crochet designers with freebies, giveaways, and tutorials.
- **Newsletter.** Email updates with blog posts, forum highlights, and Crochetville news.

## How do I use the Crochetville forum?

The forum is the heart of the community. It uses classic forum software with categories and threads.

- **Browse categories.** Topics are organized into sections like general crochet discussion, help and troubleshooting, finished objects, and crochet-alongs.
- **Post a new thread.** Click to start a new discussion. Use a descriptive title so others know what your thread is about.
- **Share photos.** You can upload images of your projects directly. The community is encouraging and loves to see what you are making.
- **Ask for help.** If you are stuck on a pattern or technique, post in the help section. Include what you are working on and where you are stuck.

## What is the NatCroMo Blog Tour?

National Crochet Month happens every March, and Crochetville runs a Designer Blog Tour to celebrate.

- **Daily designer features.** Each day of March features a different crochet designer on the blog.
- **Freebies and giveaways.** Designers offer free patterns, discounts, and prize drawings throughout the month.
- **Community event.** The blog tour brings the crochet community together and introduces makers to new designers.

You can find past blog tours and the current year's schedule on the Crochetville blog.

## How do I follow Crochetville?

Crochetville is active on several social platforms in addition to its own site.

- **Newsletter.** Subscribe on the Crochetville website for email updates.
- **Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Pinterest.** Follow Crochetville's social accounts for additional content and community interaction.

## What tips and tricks should I know?

- **Old-school forum culture.** Crochetville's forum has a slower, more thoughtful pace than social media. Posts are detailed and conversations are supportive.
- **Use the forum search.** Before posting a new question, search the forum. Chances are someone has asked about the same topic before.
- **Participate in the blog tour.** March is the busiest and most exciting time on Crochetville. Check the blog daily during NatCroMo for new designer features.
- **Share your FOs.** The community celebrates finished objects. Posting your projects encourages others and builds your presence on the forum.
- **Be kind and supportive.** Crochetville prides itself on being a positive space. Keep comments encouraging and constructive.
