Circle is a community platform used by knitting and crochet organizations, designers, and educators to run members-only spaces. Unlike public forums or social media, Circle communities are usually tied to a membership or subscription and offer a focused environment for discussions, courses, events, and exclusive content.

**Sources:** [Circle](https://circle.so/)

## How do I get started with Circle communities?

You cannot browse Circle communities publicly. Each one requires an invitation or membership from the organization running it.

- **Join the parent organization.** Most craft-related Circle communities are a benefit of membership. For example, TKGA (The Knitting Guild Association) offers its Circle community free to all TKGA members. You join TKGA first, then get access to Circle.
- **Receive an invite link.** The organization sends you a link to join their Circle community. Click the link and create a profile.
- **Set up your profile.** Add a photo, a short bio, and your craft interests. This helps other members recognize you in discussions.

## What can I do in a Circle community?

Circle communities offer a range of features designed around community engagement.

- **Discussion spaces.** Organized into topic-based areas for asking questions, sharing progress, and talking about techniques.
- **Exclusive resources.** Members get access to reference guides, video libraries, patterns, and other content not available to the public.
- **Photo sharing.** Post pictures of your works in progress and finished objects. Community members can comment and react.
- **Challenges and events.** Many Circle communities run seasonal challenges, knit-alongs, and crochet-alongs to keep members engaged and learning.
- **Courses.** Some organizations offer online courses directly inside Circle, with lessons, assignments, and discussion tied to each module.
- **Direct messaging.** Connect with other members privately for one-on-one conversation and support.

## What makes Circle different from Discord or Reddit?

Circle serves a different purpose than public community platforms.

- **Members-only access.** Circle communities are private. You need an invitation or paid membership to join. This creates a more focused and trusted environment.
- **All-in-one platform.** A single Circle community can host discussions, courses, events, and content libraries. Discord and Reddit are primarily discussion platforms.
- **Organization branded.** Each Circle community is customized to look and feel like the organization behind it. The experience feels cohesive rather than like a generic social platform.
- **Less noise.** Because communities are smaller and focused, conversations tend to stay on topic and be more substantive.

## What are some knitting and crochet Circle communities?

- **TKGA Circle Community.** The Knitting Guild Association runs a Circle community for its members. It includes chat groups organized by interest, exclusive resources, seasonal challenges, online courses, and volunteer opportunities. Free with TKGA membership.
- **Designer communities.** Many independent knit and crochet designers use Circle to run paid subscriber communities. These often include early pattern access, stitch-alongs, and direct Q and A with the designer.

To find Circle communities, check the websites of knitting and crochet organizations you are interested in, or look for designers who mention a community on their site or social media.

## What tips and tricks should I know?

- **Check the courses tab.** Many Circle communities include online courses as part of the membership. Look for a courses or learning section to see what is available.
- **Participate in challenges.** Seasonal challenges are a great way to try new techniques and connect with other members working on the same goal.
- **Use the mobile app.** Circle has mobile apps for iOS and Android. The app makes it easy to check discussions and upload photos from your phone.
- **Introduce yourself.** Most communities have an introductions space. Posting a hello helps other members get to know you and can lead to meaningful connections.
- **Check for volunteer opportunities.** Organization-run Circle communities sometimes post calls for volunteers. This is a way to get more involved and help shape the community.
