---
title: Using Knitting Buddy
description: Guide to using Knitting Buddy, the project tracking app with row and repeat counters, project timer, pattern display, built-in ruler, flashlight, and yarn stash management for crochet and knitting
---

Knitting Buddy is a project tracker for knitters and crocheters on iOS. Despite the name, it works well for both crafts and includes a row counter, repeat counter, project timer, notes, and stash management.

## How do I get started with Knitting Buddy?

Download Knitting Buddy from the App Store. The app was originally designed for knitting but crocheters will find it equally useful.

## How do the row and repeat counters work?

Track rows with a simple tap counter. The repeat counter tracks pattern sequences separately — useful for patterns that say "repeat rows 5–8 four times."

## How does the project timer work?

A built-in timer tracks how long you spend on each project. Use it to estimate how long future projects of similar size might take, or to track your crafting hours.

## Can I view my pattern while counting?

View your pattern text directly on the counter page. You do not have to switch between your pattern and your counter — both are visible at the same time.

## How do I add notes to a project?

Add notes to each project for modification details, size information, yarn substitutions, or any other reminders.

## Can I track my needles and hooks?

Track your collection of knitting needles and crochet hooks. Log what sizes you own so you always know what is available.

## How do I manage my yarn stash?

Log your yarns with details such as brand, weight, color, and quantity. Link yarns to specific projects to track usage.

## What built-in tools does Knitting Buddy include?

- **Ruler:** Measure your work in progress directly on screen
- **Flashlight:** A full-screen flashlight for low-light crafting

## What are the best tips for Knitting Buddy?

- **Keep your pattern visible.** No screen switching needed while counting.
- **Use the project timer.** Learn how long different project types take you.
- **Log your needles and hooks.** Never buy a duplicate size.
- **Use the repeat counter.** Set your repeats and focus on stitching.
