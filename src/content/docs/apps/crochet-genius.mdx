Crochet Genius is an all-in-one app for learning and practicing crochet. It combines built-in patterns, video tutorials, project tracking tools, and reference resources in a single app for iOS and Android.

## How do I get started with Crochet Genius?

Download Crochet Genius from the App Store or Google Play. The app is free with in-app purchases. You can start learning immediately with the built-in patterns and tutorials.

## What built-in patterns does Crochet Genius include?

Crochet Genius includes a library of patterns built directly into the app, covering amigurumi, accessories, home decor, and garments. New patterns are added regularly. Each pattern includes step-by-step instructions with photos.

## What video tutorials are available?

The app includes teaching videos for common stitches and techniques. These are helpful for beginners learning the basics and for experienced crocheters trying a new technique.

## Can I import my own patterns?

Import your own patterns from PDF files or web pages. This lets you use Crochet Genius as your main pattern viewer and tracker even for patterns purchased elsewhere.

## How do I use the row counters?

Add multiple row counters to track progress on different sections of a project. Tap to count, with options for multiple counters per project.

## What crochet tools does the app include?

- **Gauge adapter:** Adjust patterns to match your personal gauge
- **Unit converter:** Convert between metric and imperial measurements
- **Yarn ball converter:** Calculate yardage when substituting yarns

## How do I track my projects?

Track your projects from start to finish. Record the pattern used, yarn details, hook size, and any modifications. The app also includes a badge system to celebrate your progress.

## What are the best tips for Crochet Genius?

- **Start with built-in patterns.** They are optimized for the in-app experience.
- **Import PDFs.** Bring in patterns from Etsy, Ravelry, or other sources.
- **Check the video library.** If you get stuck on a stitch, look here first.
- **Use the gauge adapter.** Keep your project the right size when switching yarn weights.
- **Great for beginners.** The combination of patterns, videos, and tools makes this a strong choice for new crocheters.
