Pocket Crochet is a free app for tracking your crochet projects on the go. Developed by a crocheter and a software developer, it focuses on a clean, modern design with the features that matter most during a project.

**Sources:** [Pocket Crochet](https://pocketcrochet.app/)

## How do I get started with Pocket Crochet?

Download Pocket Crochet from the App Store or Google Play. The base app is free with all core features included. Cloud storage and sync, business features (timers, customer management), and additional themes require a paid subscription.

## How do I create a new project?

Start a new project and add:
- Pattern name and designer
- Yarn details (brand, color number, batch number)
- Hook size
- Notes and modifications
- Start and end dates

Organize projects into folders and archive or delete them when finished.

## How do the row counters work?

Add multiple row counters to a single project. Each counter tracks independently for rows, repeats, sections, or pattern elements. Tap to increment or decrement.

## How do I import patterns?

Upload pattern PDFs or images directly into a project. The app makes a hidden copy so you can safely delete the original to save space.

Supported formats:
- PDF files
- Image files (photos of printed patterns, screenshots)

## What pattern reading tools are available?

- **Reading lines:** Multiple highlight lines to keep your place
- **Annotations:** Scribble, draw, or use stamps directly on your pattern
- **Zoom:** Full-screen mode and adjustable zoom
- **Screen stays on:** Configurable screen timeout while working
- **Remembers position:** Reopens to your last viewed spot

## Can I add photos to my projects?

Add reference photos and progress pictures to each project. Useful for documenting modifications or tracking color combinations.

## How do I manage my yarn stash?

Log your yarns with details like brand, color number, and batch number. Link yarns to specific projects to track usage.

## Does Pocket Crochet support cloud sync?

Optional cloud storage lets you back up your projects and sync between devices. This is a paid feature.

## What languages is Pocket Crochet available in?

Dutch, English, French, German, Japanese, Spanish, Catalan, Estonian, Icelandic, Russian, and Polish.

## What are the best tips for Pocket Crochet?

- **Use multiple row counters.** Track rows, repeats, and sections within the same project.
- **Annotate your PDFs.** Draw directly on patterns to mark your size or circle tricky instructions.
- **Free core features.** Cloud sync is the main reason to upgrade.
- **Pattern memory.** The app remembers where you left off, even between sessions.
