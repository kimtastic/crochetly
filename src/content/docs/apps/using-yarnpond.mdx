Yarnpond is a platform built specifically for knit and crochet designers to manage their pattern testing process. Think of it as a project management tool designed around testing — you can find pattern testers, sample makers, and tech editors, run your tests in one place, and keep all feedback organized.

**Sources:** [Yarnpond](https://www.yarnpond.com/)

## How do I create a Yarnpond account?

Yarnpond is free for testers and tech editors. Designers pay per test or subscribe for better value.

- **Testers (free):** Browse and apply for open pattern tests, build a testing portfolio, rate designers
- **Tech editors (free):** Create a profile, apply for editing jobs, get paid
- **Designers (paid):** Post test calls publicly or privately, select testers, gather feedback, pay per test or subscribe

Sign up at [yarnpond.com](https://www.yarnpond.com/) with your email address and choose your role. Your profile will be public, so include a short bio and links to your work.

## How do I navigate Yarnpond?

The main navigation includes:

- **Open calls:** Browse active test and tech edit opportunities
- **Features:** Overview of what each role can do
- **Pricing:** Designers can see plans and prepaid packs
- **About:** Background on the platform

Once you log in, your dashboard shows your active tests, applications, and messages depending on your role.

## How do I find pattern tests to apply for?

If you are a knitter or crocheter looking to test patterns, Yarnpond makes it easy to find opportunities.

### Browsing open calls

Visit the Open Calls page to see all current test requests. Each listing includes:
- **Pattern type** (garment, accessory, toy, etc.)
- **Craft** (knit, crochet, or both)
- **Skill level required**
- **Timeline** (application deadline and test period)
- **Yarn and materials** the designer expects testers to use

### Applying to a test

Found a test that interests you? Click through and submit your application. Designers often ask about your experience, gauge swatch information, and availability. Make sure your profile is complete before applying, as designers review applicants before selecting their testing team.

### Building your tester profile

Your profile is your resume. Include:
- **Crafts you do** (knit, crochet, Tunisian, etc.)
- **Skill level**
- **Past test experience** with links or reviews
- **Types of patterns you prefer** (garments, accessories, blankets)
- **Typical turnaround time**

A complete profile with good reviews from past designers will help you get selected more often.

## How do I post a test call as a designer?

Designers use Yarnpond to recruit testers for new patterns.

### Creating a test call

From your dashboard, create a new test call and fill in:
- **Pattern details:** Name, craft, category, skill level, sizing
- **Tester requirements:** Number of testers needed, deadlines, experience level
- **Materials:** Yarn weight, fiber, yardage, hook or needle size
- **Feedback form:** Yarnpond lets you create custom questions so testers provide the specific feedback you need

You can post tests publicly (anyone can apply) or privately (invite specific testers). Private tests are great for working with a trusted team.

### Selecting testers

Review applications as they come in. You can message applicants directly, check their profiles and past reviews, and select the testers that fit best. Yarnpond tracks applications so you can compare candidates side by side.

### Running the test

Once testing begins, Yarnpond gives you:
- **Dedicated chat rooms** for each test, so you can communicate with your testing team
- **Milestone tracking** to monitor progress (yarn received, swatch complete, pattern test in progress, finished)
- **Pattern file management** so testers can download the latest version and you can distribute corrections

### Collecting feedback

When testers complete their work and submit their feedback forms, Yarnpond organizes the results in one place. You can review responses, save notes about who you worked well with, and rate your testers.

## How do I find and hire a tech editor?

Tech editors review your patterns for clarity, consistency, and accuracy before publication. Yarnpond added tech editing in 2020.

### Posting a tech edit job

Create a tech edit job from your dashboard with details about:
- **Pattern type and length**
- **Editing needed** (technical accuracy, formatting, chart checking, etc.)
- **Timeline and budget**

Tech editors apply for jobs that match their expertise. Review their profiles, past work samples, and ratings before selecting one.

### Working with an editor

Each tech edit job includes:
- **Dedicated chat** for discussing edits
- **Document management** so you can share files back and forth
- **Payment processing** through the platform

After the job is done, you can rate and review your editor to help other designers.

## What role-based features does Yarnpond offer?

Yarnpond tailors features to each role:

| Role | Key features |
|---|---|
| Tester | Browse test calls, apply, submit feedback, build portfolio, rate designers |
| Designer | Post test calls, manage applications, run tests, collect feedback, hire tech editors |
| Tech editor | Create profile, apply for editing jobs, manage documents, get paid |

## What community features does Yarnpond offer?

Yarnpond is more focused on project management than social networking, but it includes several community-oriented features:
- **Tester portfolios:** Testers build a public history of tests they have participated in, which helps designers find reliable testers
- **Ratings and reviews:** Both designers and testers can rate each other after a test, which builds trust across the platform
- **Chat rooms:** Each test and tech edit job has its own chat area for team communication
- **Private tester pools:** Designers can invite testers they have worked with before, creating ongoing relationships

## What tips and tricks should I know?

- **Complete your profile before applying.** Designers check profiles before selecting testers. A detailed profile with past work improves your chances.
- **Apply early.** Popular test calls close quickly. Check Open Calls regularly or set up notifications.
- **Communicate early and often.** If you hit a snag while testing, let the designer know. Yarnpond's chat rooms make it easy to stay in touch.
- **Rate honestly.** Reviews help the whole community. Leave constructive feedback that helps both designers and testers improve.
- **Use private tests for returning testers.** If you have a trusted testing team, invite them directly instead of posting publicly.
- **Designers: budget for test materials.** Some designers provide yarn, while others ask testers to use their own. Clarify this in your test call to avoid confusion.
- **Tech editors: showcase your work.** Link to patterns you have edited in your profile so designers can see your editing style.
