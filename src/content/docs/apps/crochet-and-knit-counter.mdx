Crochet and Knit Counter is a straightforward row counter app for iOS. It is designed for simplicity, letting you tap to count, repeat sequences easily, and keep your screen on while you work.

**Sources:** [Crochet and Knit Counter](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/crochet-and-knit-counter/id1103338573)

## How do I get started with Crochet and Knit Counter?

Download Crochet and Knit Counter from the App Store. The app costs $1.99 with optional in-app purchases for additional features.

## How do I count rows in the app?

Tap almost anywhere on the screen to increment your row count. Swipe or tap a button to decrement. The counter is large and easy to use without looking away from your project.

### How does the repeat counter work?

Need to repeat a sequence of rows a specific number of times? The repeat counter tracks your pattern repeats separately. When you complete the repeats, it notifies you and moves on.

## Can I track multiple projects?

Create separate projects with their own counters. Name each project so you can keep track of multiple works in progress at once.

## Does the app keep my screen on?

The app keeps your screen on while you are using it. You do not have to tap the screen to wake it up between rows. The screen stays on without draining excessive battery.

## Is the app accessible for users with visual impairments?

Crochet and Knit Counter works with VoiceOver. The tap sounds provide audio confirmation that your count has been recorded.

## Can I customize the app's appearance?

Choose from multiple color themes. If you become a supporter, the app icon changes to match your selected theme.

## What are the best tips for Crochet and Knit Counter?

- **Use the repeat counter.** Set it once and let the app track repeated sequences.
- **Use screen-on mode.** Keep your phone propped up and tap without fumbling to wake the screen.
- **Use the widget.** Add the counter widget to your today view for quick access.
- **Switch between projects.** Useful if you rotate between several WIPs.
