**Sources:** [Stash2Go](https://www.stash2go.com/)

Stash2Go II is a full-featured Ravelry companion app for iOS, Android, Apple Watch, Apple TV, and the web. It is designed to work offline first, so actions are queued and synced automatically when you reconnect.

## How do I get started with Stash2Go?

Download Stash2Go II from the App Store or Google Play. Sign in with your Ravelry account. The app is free to use with ads. An optional subscription or one-time lifetime purchase removes ads.

### Free vs Premium

All features are available in the free version. The only difference is ads. Upgrade with a monthly subscription, yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

## How do I browse patterns?

Stash2Go gives you access to millions of Ravelry patterns. The search includes filters for craft, difficulty, yarn weight, and category. Browse trending patterns and discover new designers. A personalized feed (beta) recommends patterns based on your favorites, stash, and activity.

## How do I manage projects and stash?

Track projects from start to finish with:
- Project details, notes, and photos
- Yarn usage tracking
- Pattern modifications documentation
- Stash organization by brand, weight, color, and fiber

Your stash stays in sync with Ravelry. Track yarn usage and link specific yarns to projects.

## How do the row counters work?

Stash2Go includes advanced row counter features:
- Multiple simultaneous counters
- Automatic reset at maximum values
- Linked counters that increment together
- Switch counters on each increase
- Counters persist across app sessions

## Can I view PDF patterns?

View downloaded patterns with zoom, markers, and annotations. PDFs work offline once downloaded.

## How does AI yarn label scanning work?

A beta feature uses your camera to scan yarn labels and automatically fill in stash details like brand, weight, fiber, and color. Edit the results manually if needed.

## Can I access Ravelry forums?

Browse forums, create topics, and reply to threads. Replies are queued offline and sync automatically when you are back online.

## Does Stash2Go work on Apple Watch or Apple TV?

Use Stash2Go on your Apple Watch for quick row counter access without reaching for your phone. The Apple TV app lets you follow patterns on the big screen while you craft.

## What are the best tips for Stash2Go?

- **Works offline.** Do not worry about cell signal. Queue actions and they sync automatically.
- **Choose from 30 themes.** Match your preference.
- **Available in 26 languages.**
- **Consider lifetime purchase.** More economical than a subscription for long-term use.
- **Try yarn scanning.** Use the AI label scanner for quick stash additions.
