**Sources:** [kntd:discover](https://kntdapp.com/)

kntd:discover is a free mobile app that connects to Ravelry and makes it easy to browse patterns and yarns on your phone. It focuses on discovery, helping you find inspiration for your next project without the clutter of the full Ravelry website.

## How do I get started with kntd:discover?

Download kntd:discover from the App Store (iOS only). Sign in with your Ravelry account. The app is free to use.

## How do I browse patterns?

The pattern browser gives you access to millions of Ravelry patterns. Each pattern card shows the photo, designer name, and basic details.

Tap through to see:
- Full pattern photos and details
- Projects other crafters have made
- Yarn requirements and yardage
- Designer information and other patterns by the same designer

### What filters are available?

Narrow your search with filters including:
- Category (hats, blankets, garments, accessories, etc.)
- Yarn weight
- Craft type (crochet or knitting)
- Availability (free or paid)
- Other attributes

## How do I browse yarns?

Search the Ravelry yarn database to explore yarns by brand, weight, fiber content, and color.

Each yarn page shows:
- Available colorways
- Yardage and care information
- Projects made with that yarn
- Pattern suggestions for that yarn

Check if you already have a yarn in your stash and see how much yardage you need for a specific pattern.

## How do I save patterns and yarns?

Add patterns or yarns to your Ravelry favorites to find them later. Pattern PDFs can be downloaded directly in the app for offline viewing.

## What are the best tips for kntd:discover?

- **Check your stash.** Look up a pattern to see if you already have suitable yarn.
- **Try yarn-first browsing.** Start with a yarn you love and see what patterns work well with it.
- **Quick filtering.** The filter system is streamlined for mobile, making it less powerful than the Ravelry website but faster for casual browsing.
- **Free with no ads.** kntd:discover has no paid tier or subscription.
