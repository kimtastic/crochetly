title: Using Ribblr
description: Guide to using Ribblr for interactive ePatterns, pattern discovery, buying and selling designs, and connecting with the crochet community through its mobile app and web platform
---

Ribblr is a modern crafting platform built around interactive "ePatterns", which are digital patterns with built-in progress tracking, smart sizing, automatic translation, and video tutorials. It works on iOS, Android, and the web.

**Sources:** [Ribblr](https://ribblr.com/)

## How do I create an account?

Sign up at [ribblr.com](https://ribblr.com/) or download the app from the App Store or Google Play. Browsing patterns is free. Some patterns are free to download, while others are paid. Ribblr uses a daily free pattern system, where you can claim a limited number of free patterns each day.

## How do I browse and discover patterns?

The home feed shows trending and recommended patterns based on your interests. Use the search bar to find specific patterns, or browse by category. Filter patterns by craft type (crochet, knitting, sewing), skill level, and more.

### What information is on a pattern page?

Each pattern page shows:
- Photos of the finished project
- Materials needed (yarn weight, hook size, notions)
- Skill level and estimated time
- Reviews and ratings from other crafters
- A preview of the ePattern format

## What are interactive ePatterns?

The ePattern format is Ribblr's standout feature. Unlike a static PDF, ePatterns include:
- **Progress tracking:** Mark rows or sections as complete as you go
- **Smart sizing:** Select your size and the pattern adjusts automatically
- **Translation:** Translate patterns into multiple languages
- **Built-in videos:** Watch technique tutorials without leaving the pattern
- **Charts and diagrams:** Interactive visual guides that zoom and scroll

Your progress is saved across devices so you can pick up where you left off.

## How do I buy and sell patterns?

### As a buyer

Purchase patterns directly through Ribblr. Paid patterns are stored in your library and available for download anytime. Claim free patterns (typically one per day on the free tier).

### As a designer

Ribblr's Ribbuild tool lets you create and sell patterns in the ePattern format.

Key features for designers:
- Upload your pattern instructions and Ribbuild handles formatting
- Automatic pattern translation and unit conversion
- Smart sizing support
- Set your own fee using $elFee (you control the platform fee percentage)
- Your patterns remain yours, since Ribblr does not claim exclusivity

## What community features does Ribblr offer?

Ribblr includes a social feed where you can share your projects, follow other crafters, and comment on patterns. Community meetups and events are organized through the platform.

## What are the best tips for Ribblr?

- **Check for daily free patterns.** A new free pattern is available each day.
- **ePattern vs PDF.** ePatterns work best within Ribblr. You can also import PDF patterns and Ribblr will try to convert them.
- **Accessibility.** Ribblr includes dark mode, reduce motion, and left-hand mode in its accessibility menu.
- **Mobile first.** The app works on any device, but the mobile experience is especially polished.
